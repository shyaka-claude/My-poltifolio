About

From engineering to digital creation.

My professional journey started with Mechanical Engineering, where I learned to look at problems from different angles, understand how things work, pay attention to details, and find practical ways to build solutions.

But somewhere along the way, I became curious about another kind of creation the digital world.

I started wondering how websites were built, how software could turn an idea into something people could interact with, and how technology was changing the way we work and communicate. That curiosity led me to programming, web development, and design.

>I didn't leave engineering behind. I built on it.

Engineering taught me how to think and solve problems.
Coding taught me how to turn ideas into working solutions.
Design taught me how to communicate those ideas visually.
Writing taught me how to put thoughts into words.

Together, these disciplines have shaped the way I see my work.

I believe your starting point doesn't have to determine where you end up. You can learn something new, change direction, and still carry everything you've learned with you.

That's how I've approached my own journey. I keep learning, experimenting, and exploring because technology never stands still and neither should we.

One of the people who influenced my journey into web development is Jennifer Niederst Robbins. Her books and resources helped me build a stronger foundation and, more importantly, taught me to look beyond simply writing code. They helped me understand how the web works and why thoughtful design matters.

>Beyond the screen

When I'm not coding or designing, you'll probably find me writing, reading, researching, or exploring new ideas. I especially enjoy books about psychology and personal development. Writing gives me a place to think, express myself, and turn ideas into words.

And music is almost always somewhere in the background. It keeps me energized, inspired, and sometimes simply makes the work more enjoyable.

I'm still learning, still experimenting, and still figuring out where this journey will take me.

And that's what I enjoy most about it.
